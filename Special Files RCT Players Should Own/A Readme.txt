Special Files every RCT Player should own

Contents:

CSS0.DAT -- Unlocks all parks in RCT1 
NOTE: Back Up any existing CSS0.DAT file first if you want to later revert back to your current progress. Place in
C:\Users\yourusername\AppData\Local\VirtualStore\Program Files (x86)\GOGcom\RollerCoaster Tycoon Deluxe\Data

SC21.SC4 -- Mega Park scenario file for RCT1 -- Place in
C:\Program Files (x86)\GOGcom\RollerCoaster Tycoon Deluxe\Scenarios
You can also open in OpenRCT2 to play immediately.

RECORDS.IDX -- Unlocks all parks in RollerCoaster Tycoon Classic 
NOTE: Back up any existing RECORDS.IDX file first if you want to later revert back to your current progress. Place in
[My Documents Folder]\Atari\RollerCoaster Tycoon Classic

tycoon park.sea -- Tycoon Park scenario file for RCT Classic -- Open in OpenRCT2 to play immediately.

Mr. Bones Wild Ride - Original Scenario.SV6 -- Original Mr. Bones Wild Ride RCT2 file from 4chan -- Open in OpenRCT2 to play immediately.

Mr. Bones Wild Ride - Full 4chan Post Archive -- Not part of the game, just for your reading pleasure. Explains the story of the Mr. Beast Wild ride.

SC34.SC4 -- Taiwan Park RCT1 Taiwanese special park -- Open in OpenRCT2 to play immediately.





Note: Depending on differences in installations, computers, and operating systems, the exact folder paths above may vary. Again I say, back up your CSS0.DAT and RECORDS.IDX before overwriting them in case you want to restore your old progress later!

All files are from original "vanilla" versions of the game mentioned for each file, unmodified by me. They should work in their respective vanilla game, as long as you have the all of the expansion packs (or GOG version of RCT1 and/or RCT2). Mega Park and Tycoon Park require the scenario to be unlocked, either with your own progress or my records files. All other scenarios do not need unlocking.
